Student support

Identify difficulties early. Respond with support.

I use assessment patterns and participation records to plan mock tests, revision material, booster sessions and individual follow-ups.

01

Monitor

Review assessment submissions, class participation and inactive student records to identify who needs assistance.

02

Intervene

Provide booster sessions, consultations, mock tests, detailed guidance and tutor referrals where needed.

03

Reflect and adjust

Use results, recurring misconceptions and student questions to revise examples, explanations and assessment preparation.
