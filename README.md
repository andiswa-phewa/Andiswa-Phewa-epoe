# Andiswa Phewa ePortfolio

Multi-page professional portfolio retaining the original cream, green and editorial typography theme.

## Main navigation
Home, Teaching, Student Work, Assessment, Mentorship, Student Success, Research and Contact.

Additional pages: About, Software Projects, Data & Analytics and Experience (accessible from the footer).

## Files
- `index.html`: homepage
- `assets/css/styles.css`: responsive styling, video treatments and subtle animations
- `assets/js/main.js`: navigation, scroll reveals, filters, contact form and synchronised video-backdrop playback
- `assets/videos/`: Algoa FM, mentoring and student demonstration videos
- `assets/images/`: original photos and branded video stills
- `assets/evidence/`: SQAT6322 assessment evidence
- `assets/docs/`: CV

## Publishing
Extract and upload the contents to the root of the `main` branch in GitHub. Ensure `index.html` appears at root. Configure Pages to deploy from `main` / `/(root)`.

## Content and accessibility
The video players use branded blurred backdrops instead of black bars. They have descriptive Play controls and preserve the whole foreground frame. The existing scroll reveals and gentle hover interactions respect reduced-motion settings.

Before publishing photos of students or their work publicly, check permissions and institutional publication rules.
